Our Story

About the Guild

A community built around the love of wood, craft, and the satisfaction of making something with your own hands.

How it started

From a Single Workshop to a Growing Community

The Wood Guild started simply — a small group of woodworkers who met at a local workshop and realized they all had the same problem: great knowledge scattered across too many places, and no single space to share it locally.

We wanted somewhere to post what we were building, talk about the tools we trusted, and point newcomers toward the best places to buy good lumber without getting overwhelmed. So we built it.

Today the Guild is home to woodworkers of every background — hobbyists building their first cutting board, seasoned craftsmen with decades of shavings on the floor, and everyone in between. What unites us is simple: a love of wood and the quiet satisfaction of making something that lasts.

What we stand for

Our Mission

To bring local woodworkers together by sharing projects, knowledge, and resources — so that anyone, at any skill level, can grow as a craftsperson and find the community they need to keep building.

What guides us

Our Values

🪵

Craftsmanship

We believe in doing things right — taking the time to fit a joint properly, sharpen a blade fully, and choose the right wood for the job. Quality over speed, always.

🤝

Community

No one learns woodworking alone. We share what we know freely — tips, mistakes, and hard-won lessons — because a stronger community makes every builder better.

🌲

Sustainability

We encourage responsible sourcing — buying from local mills, choosing reclaimed lumber when possible, and respecting the material that makes our craft possible.

📐

Learning

Every project is a chance to learn something new. We welcome beginners, celebrate growth, and believe that the best woodworkers are always still students of the craft.
